    About this map

    2,176,483 buildings of Lagos State, one for each home in the 2025 housing-stock estimate, coloured by period and set in the history that shaped them.

    A non-commercial project, made out of curiosity and for education.

    Made by Progress Peter Akpan, GIS and geospatial analyst, Lagos.

    Inspired by Alex Barakov's LAYRS: Amsterdam and Sasha Bogachev's Paris, building by building. The 3D building renderer adapts parts of Barakov's open-source viewer (MIT licence).

    Is it really 2.2 million?

    Yes. Google's building dataset alone finds between 2.17 and 2.52 million buildings inside Lagos State's 20 LGAs, depending on the confidence cut-off. In the merged footprints shown here, fewer than 0.1% overlap one another. Lagos State covers about 3,500 km², and most of its buildings are one- and two-storey compounds.

    Data

    • Footprints: Overture Maps buildings, release 2026-09-23 (OpenStreetMap, Google Open Buildings, Microsoft ML Buildings). ODbL and CC BY 4.0.
    • Year a building first appears, 2016–2023, and most heights: Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal. CC BY 4.0.
    • Year the surrounding area was first built up: GHSL built-up surface 1975–2020 (European Commission, JRC) and World Settlement Footprint Evolution 1985–2015 (DLR).
    • LGA boundaries, land, water, roads, place names: Overture Maps divisions, base, transportation and places.
    • Stories: Wikipedia and the sources linked on each one.
    • “Google Maps” opens the place in Google Maps; nothing from Google is copied into this page. “Panorama” drops you to street level inside this 3D model.

    Periods and housing stock

    The five periods and their totals come from the 2025 State of Lagos Housing Market Report: 325,000 homes before 1976, 457,119 from 1976 to 2000, 376,813 from 2001 to 2010, 88,656 from 2011 to 2015 and 928,895 from 2016 to 2025, a total of 2,176,483. The 1976 figure is derived from the report's assumed growth of 3.92% a year.

    Method

    Lagos has no public register of construction years, so every building is ranked by how early satellites show it or its surroundings built up: Google's yearly building maps for 2016–2023, the 30 m World Settlement Footprint from 1985, and the 100 m GHSL built-up layer from 1975. Within one satellite date, buildings in blocks that were already denser count as older. The oldest 325,000 fill “Before 1976”, the next 457,119 fill 1976–2000, and so on, so each period holds exactly the report's number. The report counts homes and the map counts buildings, so the match is by design rather than by survey. The 53,445 footprints least visible to the satellites are left out to make the total 2,176,483. Buildings with a documented date, such as Independence House (1961), keep it.
